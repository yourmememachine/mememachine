What RugCall is

RugCall turns rug evidence into a market: bet on whether a freshly launched Solana token gets hard-flagged by the evidence engine within 24 hours, with markets that open, trade, resolve, and pay out on Solana devnet. The evidence shown on this page is recorded snapshots of mainnet evidence, not live mainnet reads.

Resolution rule: markets resolve on hard triggers H1–H5 only. Soft scores never resolve a market. Trigger event timestamps — not detection times — decide boundary cases.

Fail-closed: if evidence cannot be obtained at expiry, the market is not resolved on a guess. Resolution defers to a 24h grace window, and after the grace window anyone can cancel the market so every stake is refunded in full.

Collateral is dUSDC, a devnet play stablecoin with no real value. Evidence shown: recorded snapshots · Markets: Devnet.

How resolution works, and the honest limits

How resolution works: a resolver watches open markets, evaluates each subject mint through the evidence module, and posts the outcome together with the sha256 of a published evidence bundle. Anyone can recompute the hash from the bundle and re-derive the verdict. The devnet program cannot read mainnet evidence on-chain; the on-chain artifact is the evidence hash, and verification is off-chain and reproducible.

Resolver trust, stated plainly: resolution today runs through a single named resolver key, and the program trusts that key for the outcome. A compromised resolver could resolve a market the wrong way and direct the pool to the wrong side; it cannot pay out more than the pool holds, cannot alter anyone's stake, and cannot pay itself beyond holding a winning position like anyone else. A wrong resolution is detectable and provable after the fact from the published bundle and its on-chain hash, and a resolver that goes silent is bounded by the grace window: anyone can cancel and every stake is refunded. A multi-key quorum resolver is the documented roadmap, not part of this build.

Honest limits: devnet only; play money only; no fees; positions are hold-to-resolution; soft scores never resolve a market; partial evidence never resolves a market. The evidence module in this repo is shared by the resolver and this page. The Solana OS does not consume this module yet — the OS verdict code and this module are kept in agreement by comparison tests, and the RugCall-enabled OS build is sequenced after the Dev3pack submission.
